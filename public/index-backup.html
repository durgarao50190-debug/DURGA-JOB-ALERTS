Our No-Interview Rule

DURGA JOB ALERTS lists only recruitment notifications that have been officially checked for NO personal interview, HR interview or viva-voce at any selection stage.

Written exams, CBT, merit, physical tests, skill tests, document verification and medical examinations can be included when confirmed by the official notification.
